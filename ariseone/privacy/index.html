Privacy Policy

ASGD Partners, LLC, doing business as AriseOne · Effective date: October 9, 2026

This policy explains how ASGD Partners, LLC, doing business as AriseOne ("AriseOne", "we", "us"), collects, uses and protects information when you communicate with us through WhatsApp, e-mail, our client portal or our virtual assistant "Ari". AriseOne is an accounting, bookkeeping and tax services firm located at 329 S Dillard St, Suite 05, Winter Garden, FL 34787, United States.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information: your name, phone number, WhatsApp profile name and e-mail address.
  • Message content: the text, audio, images and documents you send to us on WhatsApp or by e-mail.
  • Service information: the business and bookkeeping records you choose to share so we can provide our services.
  • Technical information: message identifiers, timestamps and delivery status provided by WhatsApp, and access logs from our client portal.

2. How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and provide accounting, bookkeeping and tax services.
  • To schedule meetings, send reminders and share updates about your account.
  • To let our assistant "Ari" organize requests and draft replies. Replies are reviewed and approved by our staff before they are sent.
  • To keep records required by law and to protect the security of our systems.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us operate, under confidentiality obligations and only as needed:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), to send and receive WhatsApp messages.
  • Cloud hosting providers, to store data on servers dedicated to AriseOne, with encryption in transit and at rest.
  • AI service providers, to help organize and summarize messages. Your data is not used to train public or third-party AI models.

We may also disclose information when required by law, subpoena or court order.

4. Data retention

We keep messages and service records for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal and professional record-keeping requirements (generally up to 7 years for accounting records). Information that is no longer needed is deleted.

5. Security

We use encryption, multi-factor authentication, access controls and activity logs to protect your information. Access is limited to authorized AriseOne staff. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information using reasonable safeguards.

6. Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or stop receiving WhatsApp messages from us at any time:

  • Send the word STOP on WhatsApp to stop receiving messages.
  • To request access or deletion, e-mail adriano@ariseone.us with the subject "Data request", or call +1 (407) 347-0109.

We will confirm your request and complete it within 30 days, except for records we are legally required to keep.

7. Children

Our services are intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it was last changed.
